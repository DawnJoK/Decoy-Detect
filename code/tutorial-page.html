How to Copy an App Link from Google Play Store

1.

Open the Play Store App

Launch the Google Play Store application on your Android device.

2.

Search for the App

Use the search bar at the top to find the application you want to share.

3.

Go to the App's Page

Tap on the app from the search results to open its detailed page.

4.

Tap the Share Icon

On the app's page, look for the "Share" icon (usually three dots connected by lines, or an arrow pointing right) near the top right, and tap it.

5.

Select "Copy to clipboard"

From the sharing options that appear, select "Copy to clipboard" or "Copy Link". The link will then be copied to your device's clipboard, ready to be pasted.

6.

Paste the URL on our app

The copied URL is pasted in the search input field on the Decoy Detect app's main page.

7.

Click the Search button

The search button is located right next to the URL input field. Click it to initiate the analysis.

8.

Check the Result

The analysis result will be displayed in the "Analysis Results" section. The background color of the page will change to indicate the trust level:

RED: Untrusted

ORANGE: Proceed with Caution

GREEN: Trusted
