Frequently Asked Questions

What is Decoy Detect?

Decoy Detect is a tool designed to analyze URLs and provide a trust score, helping users identify potentially malicious or untrustworthy websites. It aims to protect you from phishing, scams, and other online threats.

How is the Trust Score calculated?

The trust score is determined by analyzing various factors associated with the URL, such as domain age, SSL certificate validity, presence in blacklists, content analysis, and community feedback. A higher score indicates greater trustworthiness.

Can I submit a review for a URL?

Yes, absolutely! We encourage users to share their experiences and insights by submitting reviews. This helps improve the accuracy of our trust scores and benefits the entire community. Look for the "Add a review" link in the Community Reviews section.

What should I do if a URL has a low trust score?

If a URL receives a low trust score, it is highly recommended to exercise extreme caution. Avoid entering personal information, clicking on links, or downloading files from such sites. It might indicate a phishing attempt or a malicious website.

Is Decoy Detect always accurate?

While Decoy Detect strives for high accuracy, no system is foolproof. New threats emerge constantly. Always combine our trust score with your own judgment and other security practices. If something feels off, it's best to err on the side of caution.
